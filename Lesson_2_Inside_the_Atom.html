Structure of the Atom

Scientists have discovered that atoms consist of two main parts: the nucleus and electrons

Nucleus

Atomic Components

🔴 Protons

  • • Positively charged particles (+)
  • • Located in the nucleus
  • • Determine element identity
  • • Mass ≈ 1 atomic mass unit

⚫ Neutrons

  • • Neutral particles (no charge)
  • • Located in the nucleus
  • • Provide nuclear stability
  • • Mass ≈ 1 atomic mass unit

🔵 Electrons

  • • Negatively charged particles (-)
  • • Revolve around nucleus
  • • Determine chemical properties
  • • Mass ≈ 1/1836 atomic mass unit

⚖️
Key Properties Explained

Electric Charge

⚡

The charge of a proton (+1) is equal in magnitude to the charge of an electron (-1), but opposite in type.

Mass Distribution

⚖️

The mass of an electron is negligible compared to protons and neutrons. Most atomic mass is in the nucleus.

Electrical Neutrality

🎯

Atoms are electrically neutral because the number of positive protons equals the number of negative electrons.

🤔 Give Reasons - Why these properties matter

1. Why is the atom electrically neutral?

Answer: Because the number of negative electrons equals the number of positive protons, so the total charge is zero (+ and - cancel out).

2. Why is the mass of the atom concentrated in the nucleus?

Answer: Because the mass of an electron is negligible (very small) relative to the mass of protons and neutrons in the nucleus.

3. Why does the nucleus have a positive charge?

Answer: Because it contains positive protons and neutral neutrons. Since neutrons have no charge, only the positive charge of protons contributes to the nucleus's overall charge.

4. Why do we say the nucleus is tiny compared to the atom?

Answer: If we imagine the atom as the size of a baseball field, the nucleus would only be the size of a pinhead in the center!

Lesson Summary

What You've Learned:

  • ✅ Atoms have two main parts: nucleus and electrons
  • ✅ Nucleus contains protons (+) and neutrons (neutral)
  • ✅ Electrons (-) revolve around the nucleus at high speed
  • ✅ Atoms are electrically neutral (equal + and - charges)
  • ✅ Most atomic mass is concentrated in the tiny nucleus
