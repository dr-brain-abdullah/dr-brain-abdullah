What is Matter?

Definition of Matter

Matter is everything that has mass and volume and occupies space.

Key Properties:

  • ✓ Has mass (weight)
  • ✓ Has volume (takes up space)
  • ✓ Occupies physical space

Examples of Matter

🪨 Solids: Rocks, metals, ice, wood
💧 Liquids: Water, oil, juice, mercury
💨 Gases: Air, oxygen, carbon dioxide
⚡ Plasma: Lightning, stars, neon lights

History of Atomic Discovery
Ancient Times

Greek Philosophers

Believed matter was made of small, indivisible parts called "Atoms" (meaning "cannot be cut").

Key Idea: Matter cannot be divided infinitely
Early 19th Century

Dalton's Theory

Scientist Dalton developed the first scientific theory about atoms and stated that atoms are indivisible.

Key Idea: Atoms are solid, indivisible spheres
Later Discoveries

Rutherford's Model

Made the first experimental model of the atom, discovering that atoms have internal structure.

Key Idea: Atoms have nucleus and electrons
